# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

adaptive

## Users

Individuals who want a straightforward way to organize daily tasks on Android or the web, including when they are offline.

## Product Purpose

TaskMate helps people capture, prioritize, schedule, complete, filter, and review everyday tasks. Success means the user can understand what needs attention and update their list quickly without creating an account or depending on a network connection.

## Positioning

TaskMate is a focused, local-first task manager: core task data stays available on the device and the interface keeps daily planning intentionally simple.

## Operating Context

Users manage a personal task list, assign priorities and due dates, filter tasks by status, and review overall completion progress. The Android release is distributed as a directly installable APK.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Create, edit, complete, and delete tasks.
- Set task priorities and due dates.
- Filter tasks by status and view completion progress.
- Store task data locally for offline use.
- Run on Android and the web.
- The current public Android release is version 1.0.0 and is distributed outside Google Play as an APK.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is TaskMate. Its established identity is simple, minimalist, and professional, using a warm neutral palette and a rust-red checkmark app icon. Product copy should be direct and calm.

## Evidence on Hand

- Product behavior and feature copy in `README.md` and the application source.
- App icon and launch assets in `assets/`.
- A locally built and runtime-verified release APK in `android/app/build/outputs/apk/release/app-release.apk`.
- No testimonials, customer logos, usage statistics, or third-party awards are available and none should be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- Keep everyday task management quick and understandable.
- Preserve useful behavior offline.
- Prefer focused workflows over feature accumulation.
- Make important actions accessible across phone sizes and text scales.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Interfaces must remain usable on small phones, with enlarged text, keyboard navigation on the web, visible focus states, sufficient contrast, and reduced-motion support.
